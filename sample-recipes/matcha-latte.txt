Recipe: Classic Matcha Latte

Ingredients:
- 2g (1 tsp) ceremonial or premium matcha powder
- 60ml hot water (75-80°C, not boiling)
- 180ml milk of choice (oat, whole, or almond)
- 1 tsp honey or syrup (optional)

Steps:
1. Sift matcha powder into a bowl to remove clumps.
2. Add hot water and whisk briskly in a "W" motion with a bamboo whisk (chasen) until frothy, about 15-20 seconds.
3. Warm the milk and froth it using a steamer or handheld frother.
4. Pour the matcha into a cup, then slowly add the frothed milk.
5. Sweeten to taste and stir gently.

Notes:
- Use water below 80°C to avoid a bitter taste.
- For an iced version, whisk matcha with cold water and pour over ice before adding cold milk.
