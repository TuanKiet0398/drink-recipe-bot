Recipe: Thai Milk Tea

Ingredients:
- 2 tbsp Thai tea mix (loose leaf, black tea blend)
- 240ml water
- 2 tbsp sweetened condensed milk
- 1 tbsp sugar (adjust to taste)
- Evaporated milk or half-and-half, for topping
- Ice cubes

Steps:
1. Bring water to a boil, add Thai tea mix, and steep for 5 minutes for a strong brew.
2. Strain the tea into a pitcher, discarding the leaves.
3. While hot, stir in sugar and condensed milk until fully dissolved.
4. Let the mixture cool slightly, then pour over a glass filled with ice.
5. Top with a splash of evaporated milk, creating a layered effect.
6. Stir before drinking.

Notes:
- Steep time controls strength; steep longer for a bolder tea flavor.
- Traditional Thai tea mix gets its orange color from added food coloring; use plain black tea if you prefer a natural color.
