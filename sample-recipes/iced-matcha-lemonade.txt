Recipe: Iced Matcha Lemonade

Ingredients:
- 2g matcha powder
- 30ml cold water
- 200ml fresh lemonade (lemon juice + water + sugar/simple syrup)
- Ice cubes
- Mint leaves for garnish (optional)

Steps:
1. Whisk matcha powder with cold water in a bowl until smooth and frothy.
2. Fill a glass with ice cubes.
3. Pour the lemonade over the ice, filling about 3/4 of the glass.
4. Slowly pour the whisked matcha on top so it layers over the lemonade.
5. Garnish with a mint leaf and stir before drinking.

Notes:
- The tartness of the lemonade balances matcha's natural bitterness.
- Adjust sugar in the lemonade to taste; matcha itself is unsweetened.
