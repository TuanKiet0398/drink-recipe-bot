Recipe: Honey Jasmine Green Tea

Ingredients:
- 2g jasmine green tea leaves (or 1 tea bag)
- 200ml hot water (80°C)
- 1-2 tsp honey
- A few jasmine flowers or dried jasmine buds for garnish (optional)

Steps:
1. Heat water to about 80°C (avoid boiling to preserve the tea's delicate aroma).
2. Steep the jasmine green tea leaves for 2-3 minutes.
3. Remove the leaves and stir in honey while the tea is still warm so it dissolves fully.
4. Garnish with jasmine buds if desired.
5. Serve warm, or pour over ice for an iced version.

Notes:
- Over-steeping jasmine green tea can make it taste grassy or bitter; stick to 2-3 minutes.
- For iced version, brew double-strength tea so it isn't diluted by ice.
